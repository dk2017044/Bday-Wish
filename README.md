# ✨ The Ultimate Aesthetic Birthday Surprise Website

Inspired by trending **21st.dev** components, GitHub viral birthday repositories, and Instagram/TikTok reel surprises.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **💌 Scene 0: Mystery Envelope & Golden Wax Seal Gate**
   - 3D realistic wax seal unboxing animation.
   - Dynamic recipient initial stamped on the seal.
   - Smooth unwrap sequence triggering celebratory confetti & ambient music.

2. **🎵 Ambient Lo-Fi Vinyl Music Engine**
   - Built with **Web Audio API** (zero external MP3 dependencies, guaranteed 100% offline & fast playback).
   - Spinning vintage vinyl disc, live animated equalizer audio bars, play/pause controls.
   - Procedural Happy Birthday Lo-Fi chords, warm piano tones, and celebration chimes.

3. **🎂 Interactive Birthday Cake & Dual-Mode Candle Blowing**
   - Multi-tier frosted cake with animated dripping icing, cherries, and sprinkles.
   - **Real Microphone Blow Detection**: Detects gentle air puff into the phone/mic to extinguish the flame!
   - Tap/click fallback to blow candle.
   - Extinguishing flame triggers realistic smoke dispersion, confetti shower, and birthday cheer.
   - **Cake Cutting**: Slide the knife to slice the cake and enjoy a celebratory treat.

4. **📸 21st.dev Style Hanging Clothesline Polaroid Memories**
   - Fairy lights with twinkling illumination and wooden peg clips.
   - 4 vintage Polaroid photos with 3D perspective mouse tilt.
   - **Click to Flip**: Flip cards over to read heartfelt handwritten diary notes on the back.

5. **🎟️ Scratch-to-Reveal Birthday Coupons**
   - Realistic HTML5 canvas silver & gold scratch tickets with mouse/touch scratch physics.
   - Scratch off the foil to unlock treats:
     - *Pass 1: Unlimited Free Hugs & Support 🫂*
     - *Pass 2: Midnight Food & Cafe Date ☕🍰*
     - *Pass 3: The Universal Wish Pass (Golden Ticket) 🧞‍♂️*
   - Auto-reveal & confetti celebration once >45% is scratched.

6. **📜 Heartfelt 3D Unfolding Parchment Letter**
   - Wax-sealed letter that unfolds into a warm, deeply personal letter with your customized sender name.

7. **🎈 Balloon Popping Game & 🏮 Cosmic Sky Lanterns**
   - Floating helium balloons rising up. Tap to pop them with sound effects and keep score!
   - Write your secret birthday wish and launch a glowing sky lantern into space.

8. **🎨 4 Aesthetic Color Palettes**
   - 💖 **Cupid Pink & Romantic Rose**: Romantic love aesthetic with glowing floating micro-hearts, blush peony tones, and ruby-pink wax seal.
   - 🌌 **Midnight Stardust**: 21st.dev dark cosmic glow with indigo & violet.
   - 🌅 **Golden Sunset**: Warm amber twilight and peach.
   - 🌿 **Matcha Dream**: Modern Pinterest sage & mocha.

9. **✨ Surprise Studio Customizer (Viral Sharing Engine)**
   - Click **"✨ Customize Surprise"** at any time.
   - Personalize Recipient Name, Milestone Age, Sender Name, Message, and Color Theme.
   - **Instant Shareable Link**: Generates a customized URL (with query parameters) that you can copy or send directly via WhatsApp!

---

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can open `index.html` directly in your browser or run a simple local web server:

```powershell
# Using Python
python -m http.server 8080

# Or open directly in your browser
Start-Process http://localhost:8080/
```
